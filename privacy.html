Brainlio Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Brainlio ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Brainlio is a casual "find the differences" puzzle game.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information stored only on your device

Brainlio keeps your game progress locally on your device — it is not tied to a name, email address, or account, because Brainlio does not have user accounts or logins. This includes:

  • Coins, stars, and score earned
  • Level and chapter progress, and completed daily challenges/streaks
  • Sound, music, vibration, and language preferences
  • Hint inventory and any in-app purchases you've made (see 1.3)

This data stays on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted and cannot be recovered by us, because we do not operate a server that stores it.

1.2 Information collected for showing ads

Brainlio shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation platform that in the current version of the app connects to ironSource and Unity Ads as ad networks. To select and display ads, and to measure ad performance, these services automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language, general region)
  • IP address (used to estimate general location, such as country/city — not precise GPS location)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, clicked, or completed)

If you consent to personalized ads (see the in-app consent screen shown the first time you open Brainlio), this data may also be used to show ads tailored to your interests. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

1.3 In-app purchases

Brainlio's shop may offer optional purchases (such as coin packs or a subscription). When you make a purchase, it is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Brainlio does not see or store your payment card details — we only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed, which is recorded in your local game progress (see 1.1).

1.4 What Brainlio does not currently do

Brainlio does not currently use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not require or offer account creation, login, or cloud save. If this changes in a future update, we will update this policy and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

2. Why we collect this information

  • To let you keep your progress while you're playing.
  • To show ads that keep Brainlio free to play, and to measure how those ads perform.
  • To process optional in-app purchases through the app stores.
  • To comply with legal requirements (such as honoring your ad consent choice).

4. How long we keep information

  • Local game progress: stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed, or until you clear the app's data.
  • Advertising and ad-interaction data: retained by the ad networks listed above according to their own retention policies — we don't control or store this data ourselves, since Brainlio has no server.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Opt out of personalized ads: you can decline personalized ads on the consent screen shown the first time you open the app. You can also limit ad tracking from your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization / Delete advertising ID; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage/data from your device's app settings, deletes all locally stored progress. Because we don't operate a server or account system, there is no separate "delete my account" request to make.
  • EU/UK/California residents: depending on where you live, you may have rights to know what data is processed about you, to object to its use, or (in California, under the CCPA/CPRA) to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your information for advertising purposes — the ad-personalization opt-out above covers this. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at infobrainlio@gmail.com.

6. Children's privacy

Brainlio is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent that applies where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and Brainlio does not require any personal information (name, email, etc.) to play. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at infobrainlio@gmail.com so we can address it.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information described in this policy, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add new features, ad networks, analytics, or in-app purchase options. Any changes will be reflected in the effective date at the top of this document.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how Brainlio handles information, contact us at: infobrainlio@gmail.com
